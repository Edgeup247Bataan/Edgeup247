# My style

Claude fills this in during style setup (type `start`) and copies it to `my-style.md`.
Edit `my-style.md`, not this template.

- Format: vertical 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) or horizontal 16:9 (YouTube)
- Captions: style, position and colours
- Hook text in the first 3 seconds: yes or no
- Pacing: how tight the cuts are
- Graphics and zoom-ins: how much
- Color look: natural, warm, cool, black and white, or orange and teal
- Music: always on; mood
- Sound design: always on
